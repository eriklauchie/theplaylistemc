Meet Eron Trent

Eron Trent's experience as a musician has taken him through weddings, corporate events, community festivals, concert stages, and various other musical performance settings. Those experiences shaped his ability to understand what different audiences and settings need from live music.

He started The Playlist Entertainment Music Collective to bring that versatility into one brand, creating customized live music experiences that feel right for the moment, the event, and the people in the room. The Playlist Entertainment Music Collective strives to cultivate energy that inspires unforgettable experiences through live music that connects people, evokes emotion, and turns moments into lasting memories.
